Team Calvin Racing · Reel Studio

Reel Studio is a private tool that Team Calvin Racing (@teamcalvinracing) uses on its own Mac to edit car-build videos and post them to its own social media accounts, including TikTok.

It is not a public service: nobody else can sign up or log in. It only posts videos made by Team Calvin Racing to Team Calvin Racing's own accounts, after the account owner has logged in with TikTok and approved it.

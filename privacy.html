Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Reel Studio is a private tool that Team Calvin Racing runs on its own computer. This policy explains what it does with data from TikTok.

What we get from TikTok

When the account owner logs in with TikTok, Reel Studio receives the account's basic profile (open ID, display name, username and avatar) and permission to post videos to that account (the user.info.basic and video.publish scopes).

How we use it

Only to post Team Calvin Racing's own videos to its own TikTok account and to show which account is connected. We don't read other users' data, and we don't sell, share or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Login tokens are stored encrypted in the macOS Keychain on Team Calvin Racing's computer. Post results (the post ID, link and time) are kept in a local file on that computer. Nothing is sent to anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until the account is disconnected. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions); after that the stored login no longer works and is deleted the next time Reel Studio is reconnected or reset.

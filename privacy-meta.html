Privacy Policy: Instagram and Facebook

Last updated 24 November 2026

Reel Studio is a private tool run by Team Calvin Racing for its own accounts. It isn't offered to the public and nobody else logs in to it. This policy explains what it does with data from Meta (Instagram and Facebook).

What we get from Meta

  • A login token for our own Instagram account and Facebook Page, and the names of the Pages the account owner manages.
  • Our own posts, their results and their statistics.
  • Comments on our own posts: the text, the time and the commenter's username.
  • Public information about business and creator accounts on Instagram: username, name, bio, follower and post counts, and recent public posts. This helps us write a relevant comment or reply.

How we use it

To publish our own videos and covers, see how they perform, reply to comments on our posts, and draft comments that the account owner reviews and posts by hand. We don't sell this data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Supabase hosts our posting and outreach tools. Anthropic's Claude AI drafts replies and comments, so it receives the comment or post text and the public account information above. Google and TikTok receive our own videos when we post them there; they aren't sent Meta data about other people.

Where it's stored

Login tokens are stored encrypted in the macOS Keychain on our computer and in Supabase Vault. Comment, profile and posting records are kept on our computer and in our private Supabase database.

How long we keep it

We keep records while we use the tool. The account owner can end access in Facebook under Settings, Business integrations.

Delete your data

If you commented on our posts, or we hold public information about your account, message @teamcalvinracing on Instagram from that account and ask us to delete it. We'll remove it from our records.
